# Legal-review checklist — draft regulatory rules

For the organisation's legal / compliance reviewer. **Every rule below is a draft.** SALEEM does not activate
SAMA, CMA or Insurance Authority rules: a draft only runs for an organisation that opts in to draft rules, and its
findings are always capped at «تنبيه» and labelled as a draft rule. Activation happens only after this review, as a
new rulebook release published by a platform admin (`v2_publish_rulebook`). Nothing in SALEEM is legal advice.

## How to review a rule
1. Open the rule in «القواعد» (code, Arabic text, the instruction the model receives, and the source article).
2. Compare it with the official text of the cited article. Check that the wording, scope, product types, channels
   and severity match the regulation. Nothing may be added that the regulation does not say.
3. Record the outcome in the last column: approve as is, approve with changes (write them), or reject.
4. A platform admin publishes the approved rules as a new release; earlier reports keep the release they used.

## Packs
| Pack | Status | Before activation |

| `sa-sama-advertising` (SAMA advertising rules) | draft | Review every rule below against the current published SAMA rules. |

| `sa-ia-insurance` (Insurance Authority) | no rules | Confirm the Insurance Authority's **current** advertising requirements first; no rule is written until then. |
